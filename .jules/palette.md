## 2026-09-05 - Add ARIA Labels to Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found multiple icon-only buttons (bell, theme toggle, timer controls) missing aria-labels and tooltips, which makes them inaccessible to screen readers and potentially confusing to visually abled users on hover.
**Action:** Always add aria-label and title (in Turkish) to buttons that do not contain visible text content to ensure accessibility and better user experience.
